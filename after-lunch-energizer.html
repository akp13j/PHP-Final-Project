⚡ AFTER LUNCH ENERGIZER
👥 53 trainees
5–15 MINUTES · NO SLEEPING 😴🚫

Wake up.
Laugh. Compete.

A fast classroom game designed for the post-lunch dip. Project this page and let everyone play together.

🎮 How it works

Split 53 trainees into 7–9 teams. Choose a game round, put the timer on the screen, and let teams shout or write their answers. The trainer controls the page.

Recommended flow: 30-second movement → Emoji Blitz → This-or-That → Speed Word → final team challenge.

🏃

30-Second Reset

Stand up, move, copy the trainer, and reset the room.

😂

Emoji Blitz

Guess the data/reporting concept from emojis.

⚔️

This or That

Teams choose between two funny options.

⚡

Speed Word

Describe a concept without saying the forbidden words.

🏆

Final Face-Off

Three rapid questions. First team to 3 points wins.

📊

Team Scoreboard

Keep the competition visible.
